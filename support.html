Common questions

How do I reset all my data?

Open the app, tap the Budget tab (gear icon at the bottom), scroll to the bottom, tap Reset All Data. Confirm. Everything's gone.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

Yes. All data is stored on your device. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your data may be in the encrypted backup, but I have no access to recover it.

Can I export my data?

Yes, with Pro. Budget tab → Export, choose CSV or PDF. The CSV opens cleanly in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets.

Does it sync across devices?

Yes, with Pro Couples / Family Mode. Pair up to 5 devices via a 6-digit code; sync runs over Apple's encrypted iCloud. Solo mode is single-device only — no sync, no servers, fully offline.

What does Pro include?

  • Can I Afford This? — instant yes/no on any purchase
  • Daily Spend Nudge — your safe daily spend, every morning
  • Couples & Family Mode — share one budget across up to 5 phones
  • Smart alerts (8 types) and spending forecasts
  • Unlimited categories and full history
  • Recurring bills, savings goals
  • CSV / PDF export, custom budget periods

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Daily Budget → Cancel Subscription. No questions, no friction. You'll keep Pro until the end of the current billing period.

Lifetime plan — is it really lifetime?

Yes. One payment, you keep every Pro feature forever, including any future Pro features I ship. The Lifetime plan also has Family Sharing enabled, so up to 5 family members get Pro on their own Apple IDs.

My free trial started but Pro features aren't showing up.

Try: app Settings → tap Restore Purchases in the Pro modal. Apple sometimes takes 30–60 seconds to register a new subscription. If it still isn't working after 5 minutes, email me with your Apple ID email and I'll check on the RevenueCat side.

I want a refund.

App Store refunds go through Apple, not me. Open reportaproblem.apple.com on a browser, sign in with your Apple ID, find Daily Budget in your purchase list, click Report a Problem, pick a reason. Apple processes most refunds within 48 hours.
