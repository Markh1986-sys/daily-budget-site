Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 May 2026
Last updated: 12 May 2026
Developer: Mark Holmes · markh1986@hotmail.com

The short version

Daily Budget runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or financial data. Everything you enter — budgets, transactions, categories, goals, pay date, pay-period totals — stays on your iPhone and never leaves it.

What we collect

Nothing. Daily Budget does not collect any personal information, financial data, usage analytics, device identifiers, location, contacts, or any other information about you or your use of the app.

What we store

All data you enter into Daily Budget is stored locally on your device using iOS native storage (UserDefaults / Core Data / Capacitor Preferences). If you delete the app, all of your data is permanently deleted with it. We have no way to recover it.

What we share

Nothing. Because we collect no information, there is nothing to share. We do not sell, rent, license, or otherwise disclose any data to advertisers, analytics companies, marketing partners, or anyone else.

Couples and Family Mode

If you choose to use the optional Couples / Family sharing feature in Pro, your budget data syncs between paired devices through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Apple encrypts this end-to-end using your iCloud account. The data does not pass through our servers — we have no servers. We cannot see your shared budget data and cannot access it.

In-app purchases

If you choose to subscribe to Daily Budget Pro or buy a Lifetime unlock, the purchase is handled by Apple and validated via RevenueCat (our subscription-management provider). RevenueCat sees an anonymous app-user ID, the product purchased, and the country of purchase, in order to manage your entitlements across reinstalls. RevenueCat does not see your name, email, financial data, or any of the data you enter into the app. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Third-party services

Other than RevenueCat for purchase management, Daily Budget does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

Children's privacy

Daily Budget is suitable for users of all ages. Because we collect no information, we do not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.

Backups

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, iOS may include Daily Budget's local data in your encrypted iCloud backup. This is handled entirely by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to these backups.

Your rights

Because we do not collect, store, or process any personal information about you on our servers, common data-protection rights such as access, deletion, correction, or portability are managed entirely by you on your device. You can view and edit any data inside the app, reset all data via Settings, or permanently delete all app data by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If we update this privacy policy, the "Last updated" date at the top will change and the new version will be posted at this URL.
