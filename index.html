What it does

The free version is the one you'll open every morning. Pro adds the smarter pieces — only when you want them.

Daily check-in

Open the app. See yesterday, see today, see your week. Less than a minute. Free forever.

Can I afford this?

At the till? Ask the app. Get a real yes/no based on your remaining budget, upcoming bills, and days to payday.

Daily spend nudge

Every morning: exactly how much you can spend today and stay on budget. No guesswork.

Pay-cycle aware

Paid weekly? Fortnightly? On the 28th? Budgets reset on payday, not on the 1st.

Couples & family

Share one budget across both phones via encrypted iCloud sync. Both log spending; both see the total.

Truly private

No accounts. No analytics. No trackers. Data lives on your phone. We don't have your data because we don't collect it.
